Mobile behavior

Under 960 px the dock stacks into one sticky column: time readouts, then the full-width timeline, then transport.

  • Band labels hide; the phase name stays next to the simulated time.
  • The whole track is the native range, so touch drag and keyboard arrows share one control.
  • Scrubbing or a preset switches to Manual and pauses auto until you press Resume.
  • With reduced motion on, the dock starts paused at your PC time; after Resume the playhead steps once per second.
