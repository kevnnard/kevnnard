Mobile behavior

Under 960 px the console spans the top edge and starts collapsed to a one-line pill: simulated time, phase and mode. Tapping it opens the full instrument over the room.

  • The pill is the native details summary, so Enter and Space toggle it.
  • Mode is one three-state switch: Auto runs, Pause holds, Manual holds and hands the time to you.
  • Scrubbing, Jump to or a preset selects Manual; choosing Auto continues from the shown time, Sync to PC restarts from your PC clock.
  • With reduced motion on, the console starts on Pause at your PC time; after Auto the readout steps once per second.
