Mobile behavior

Under 960 px the controller becomes a sticky bottom sheet so the room keeps the top of the screen.

  • The dial shrinks to 200 px; the native range stays as the precise and keyboard path.
  • Presets fold behind one button to keep the sheet one row tall.
  • Dragging the dial or the range switches to Manual and pauses auto until you press Resume.
  • With reduced motion on, the clock starts paused at your PC time and only runs after you press Resume; values then update once per second, with no smooth sweep.
