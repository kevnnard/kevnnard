Night gate

Lights follow the simulated clock, not the PC clock. They can be on from 18:00 through 05:59 and are always off from 06:00 through 17:59. Each light also has its own switch: a disabled light stays off at night.

Rail and detail

  • The rail lists sections (Lights, Schedule); Arrow Up/Down, Home and End move between them.
  • Pick a light with the arrow keys in the list; the editor below always shows one light in full.
  • Collapse (or Escape inside the panel) folds it down to the rail; choosing a section opens it again.

Mobile

Under 960 px the rail turns into a tab row along the top edge and the panel starts collapsed to that row. The lower-left day controller stays in place below the room.
