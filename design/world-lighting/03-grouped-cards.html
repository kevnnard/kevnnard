Swatch colors are scene data you pick for each light, not theme colors. New scene settings arrive as their own cards in this stack.

Night gate

Lights follow the simulated clock, not the PC clock. They can be on from 18:00 through 05:59 and are always off from 06:00 through 17:59. Each light also has its own switch: a disabled light stays off at night.

Grouped cards

  • Cards group lights by where they sit in the room, and every control is visible without opening anything.
  • Tab order runs gate, then each light: switch, color, intensity.
  • Hide settings folds the stack to its header line; the button keeps focus.
