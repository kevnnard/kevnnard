Night lights

Swatches are scene data you pick for each light, not theme colors.

New scene settings are added here as their own sections.

Night gate

Lights follow the simulated clock, not the PC clock. They can be on from 18:00 through 05:59 and are always off from 06:00 through 17:59. Each light also has its own switch: a disabled light stays off at night.

Compact accordion

  • Same chassis as the time console: one-line summary, collapses with Enter or Space.
  • Sections open independently; inside Night lights only one light is open at a time.
  • Arrow Up/Down, Home and End move between light headers.

Mobile

Under 960 px the panel spans the top edge and starts collapsed to its summary line, so the room and the lower-left day controller stay visible. Opening it scrolls inside a 70% height sheet.
